# Library of Faith

Classic books and documents of the Christian faith — public domain, made easy to read on the web, chapter by chapter.

**Read it live:** https://jesuslovestyler.github.io/Library-of-Faith/

First volume: *Creeds & the Didache* — the Apostles' Creed, the Nicene Creed, and the Athanasian Creed (from Schaff's *Creeds of Christendom*, 1877) and all sixteen chapters of the Didache (Roberts–Donaldson, *Ante-Nicene Fathers*, 1885). More books are being prepared: Sibbes's *The Bruised Reed*, *The Imitation of Christ*, Bunyan's *The Pilgrim's Progress* Part I, and Edwards's "Heaven Is a World of Love."

Every text is public domain in both its edition and its translation, with the source named on its page. Copy and share freely.
